Likability dashboard

Change = a student's rating of their partner after the cooperation tower minus their rating after the competition game. Positive means they liked the partner more after cooperating. Only students with both ratings count toward the change.

Distribution of change

Every rating, one row per student

Partner 1 and Partner 2 are the two students in a group. "Rater" is the student giving the rating; "Partner" is the student they rated. Group numbers repeat across sessions, so always read the Session column.
